# SevaSetu (सेवासेतु) — 2-Minute Pitch Script

> **Speaking Time:** ~2 Minutes (~280 words at a natural, conversational pace)  
> **Core Focus:** Real-world value added for Doctors, Nurses, and Patients

---

## 0:00 – 0:25 | The Problem Everyone Here Has Faced
"Think about the last time you or your parents visited a busy clinic or hospital.  
Wait times are long, and when you finally sit in front of the doctor, the consultation lasts barely **5 minutes**.  

Yet **half of that precious time is wasted** on basic, generic questions: *'Since when do you have fever? Is it worse at night? What medicines are you already taking?'*—questions that don't actually need a doctor's specialized expertise.  

And then, when you walk out with a prescription full of medical shorthand like `OD`, `BD`, `TDS`, two things happen at home: patients don't actually know **what each medicine is used for**, and they either forget doses or get confused between old and new prescriptions."

---

## 0:25 – 0:40 | Introducing SevaSetu
"That is why we built **SevaSetu (सेवासेतु)**—which in Sanskrit means a **'Bridge of Compassionate Care.'**  
Powered by **Gemma 4**, SevaSetu adds real value at the two moments that matter most: **Before Your Visit** and **After Your Visit**."

---

## 0:40 – 1:15 | Value #1: Before Your Visit — Saving Doctor & Patient Time
"First, **Before Your Visit**, while the patient is at the waiting bay—either the **patient themselves or the nurse at the bay** can enter the initial problem using **Voice or Text, in Hindi or English**.  

Immediately, Gemma 4 understands their symptoms and asks **3 to 4 smart follow-up questions as simple text on screen**—covering all those routine, generic questions *before* the patient ever steps into the doctor's room.  

**The value here is huge:**
* **For patients:** No language barrier—they can speak naturally in Hindi or English.
* **For doctors:** They get a clean, 15-second summary right on their screen and **save 3 to 4 minutes per consultation**. Instead of acting like data-entry clerks asking basic questions, doctors can spend 100% of their time on diagnosis and care."

---

## 1:15 – 1:45 | Value #2: After Your Visit — Clarity & Smart Medicine Scheduling
"Second, **After Your Visit**, patients don't have to type a single medicine name manually. They simply **scan their prescription**.  

Gemma 4 reads the prescription and adds three immediate layers of value:
1. **Plain-Language Clarity:** Right in the app UI, the patient sees **what each medicine is actually used for** in simple everyday language—so they aren't blindly swallowing pills.
2. **Smart Missing-Days Check:** Doctors often forget to write clearly how many days a medicine should be taken. If the days of dosage are unclear on the scan, SevaSetu automatically prompts the user to confirm the days manually.
3. **Smart Google Calendar Sync:** With one tap, SevaSetu schedules every dose into **Google Calendar**—and automatically **removes any old medicine events** from previous prescriptions, so there is never confusion or accidental double-dosing."

---

## 1:45 – 2:00 | Closing — Why SevaSetu Matters
"Notice what SevaSetu does *not* do: it doesn't try to replace the doctor or play AI doctor.  
Instead, it **saves the doctor's time before the visit**, and **protects the patient's recovery after the visit**.  

And because this is **Hacktoberfest**, we built SevaSetu so anyone here can contribute new Hindi/English symptom templates and medicine explainers in a single Pull Request.  

**SevaSetu: Walk in prepared. Walk out knowing how to heal.** Thank you!"

---

## Quick 3-Point Cheat Sheet (If Asked by Judges)
1. **Value to Doctors:** Saves 50–60% of consultation time by finishing generic history-taking at the nurse/patient bay before the visit.
2. **Value to Nurses & Clinics:** Voice or Text intake in **Hindi & English**, turning spoken complaints into structured clinical briefs.
3. **Value to Patients:** Zero manual typing (just scan the prescription), see **what each medicine is used for** in the UI, fill in missing dosage days if unclear, and get automatic **Google Calendar reminders** that clean up old prescription events.
